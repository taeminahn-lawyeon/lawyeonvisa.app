Legal support agreement with Chosun University for international students

Venue Chosun UniversityRead ~3 min

Contents

Legal support agreement

On 10 April 2026, Law Firm Lawyeon Immigration Law Center signed a memorandum of understanding with the Office of External Cooperation at Chosun University to provide legal support for international students.

The two institutions agreed to cooperate on education about Korean law and legal services, including changes of immigration status.

Areas of cooperation

1
Legal education for international students
Education on Korean laws relevant to student life, including the Immigration Control Act.
2
Legal support including visa changes
Assistance with visa matters, including changes from D-2 student status after graduation, and other legal services.
3
Legal issues during study
Joint assistance with legal issues concerning immigration status and daily life in Korea.

Special lecture on Korean law

On the same day, Lawyeon gave a lecture on Korean law for international students at Chosun University.

The lecture covered Korean laws relevant to students, including the Immigration Control Act, and visa options after graduation. Topics included maintaining lawful status while studying and changing immigration status to pursue a career after graduation.

Special lecture on post-graduation visa pathways and Korean law, including the Immigration Control Act (10 Apr 2026)

Continuing legal support

Lawyeon’s attorneys and immigration specialists will provide legal education and visa advice under the agreement. Their support will cover immigration issues during study and changes of status for students pursuing careers after graduation.
