Foreign National Investigated in a Drug Delivery Case: No Prosecution for Alleged Drug Use

Outcome No prosecution for alleged drug use

Law Firm Lawyeon represented a Vietnamese national investigated in connection with drug delivery and secured a decision not to prosecute the allegation of drug use.

The client became involved in drug delivery through acquaintances while staying in Korea without lawful immigration status. During police questioning, the client had difficulty explaining their circumstances in Korean and had made an admission concerning drug use.

Attorneys S.H. Bong and S.C. Kim met with the client and attended questioning from the early stages of the investigation. Lawyeon worked with interpreters to understand the client’s account and statements and respond to the investigation.

For the drug-use allegation, counsel presented the negative preliminary drug test result and argued that there was no objective evidence supporting drug use beyond the client’s admission. Relying on the test result and the absence of supporting evidence, counsel submitted that the allegation was insufficiently substantiated.

The investigating authority accepted counsel’s submissions and decided not to prosecute the drug-use allegation. By challenging the evidence of drug use within a case involving drug delivery, the defense narrowed the scope of the client’s criminal exposure.

In the trial proceedings relating to drug delivery, attorney D.H. Nam presented the treatment needs of the client’s young child in Vietnam, the client’s financial circumstances, and the immigration consequences of criminal punishment as mitigating factors.
